How to type

  • Type each character the way you read it, in 中文 or ASCII. A character has no width of its own, so press space after it: 名九勾五 中七挑三 or s9k5 d7u3 → s9k5 d7u3. Or type # before it: #名九勾五#中七挑三 or #s9k5#d7u3 → #s9k5#d7u3
  • In ASCII, most characters are spelled with their pinyin or its initials, like 泛音 (which goes first): 泛音名九挑六 or fanyins9u6 or fys9u6 → fys9u6. The main fingerings sit where your fingers rest on a QWERTY keyboard. The left hand's fingers are on the left, s 名, d 中 and f 食, with v 大 and x 跪 below. The right hand's strokes are on the right, h 托, j 抹, k 勾 and l 打, with u 挑, i 剔 and o 摘 above and n 劈 below.
  • Hui and strings are numbers, and .5 shows as 半: 中八五勾五 or d8.5k5 → d8.5k5
  • A comma keeps a hui and a string apart: 名七，六 or s7,6 → s7,6
  • A chord puts | between its left and right sides. 撮大九，六｜散三 or Cv9,6|03 → Cv9,6|03
  • A word written down the side (上, 下, 泛起…) starts with a colon. Chinese is safest here, as some pinyin spellings are alike. ：上七九 or :shang79 → :shang79. A coming version of the font should let you leave the colon out.
  • In Chrome (or if you see overlapping characters), type the word in Chinese: 带起 rather than daiqi. While you type in ASCII, Chrome can redraw only the end of a word. Cutting the text and pasting it back puts it right.
More rules: open strings, 外, 急 绰 注 and numbers
  • An open string is 散 or 0. 散勾三 or 0k3 → 0k3
  • Past the 13th hui is 外 or 13+. 名外挑六 or s13+u6 → s13+u6
  • 急 >, 绰 / and 注 \ go after the hui. 名九绰勾六 or s9/k6 → s9/k6
  • 名七六 or s76 → s76 7.6 hui, no string.
  • 名七六六 or s766 → s766 7.6 hui, string 6.
  • 历七六 or U76 → U76 strings 7 and 6: digits only make a decimal right after a left-hand finger.
  • 名七点九 → 名七点九 In Chinese, 点 or 徽 between the digits also marks the decimal.
  • 就勾五 or jiuk5 → 就勾五 就 repeats the left hand of the character before.
  • Keep each character in one script: 勾3 or k三 may show in a browser but not in Word.
Using the font in Sibelius or MuseScore

There the spacebar moves lyrics to the next note. End each character with + instead to give it its width, then press space to move on.

  • 勾七+ or k7+ → k7+
  • ：泛起+名九挑六 or :fanqi+s9u6 → :fanqi+s9u6 one word, on one note.
