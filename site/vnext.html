1. Vertical words without the colon

A word with a character only a vertical word can hold (上, 下, 退, 分开, 少息, 带起…) is set vertically without typing the colon: shang34 gives the same as :shang34. The colon still works, and words that need it to stand vertically (:lun, :0) still do.

2. Hui numbers in Chinese

  • 十一, 十二 and 十三 are always hui 11, 12 and 13. 10.2 is written 十徽二, 十徽二分 or 十点二. 十四 to 十九 and 十半 are still 10.4 to 10.9 and 10.5.
  • 徽 is a decimal point, as 点 is: 十徽二 is 10.2 and 七徽六 is 7.6. 分 may close the decimal (十徽二分). 徽 with nothing after it closes the number: 十徽 is 10.
  • 徽 before a 半 that starts a word ends the number, as a comma does: 名九徽半轮六 is 9, then 半轮. Without 徽, 9.5 comes first: 名九半轮六 is 9.5, then 轮.

3. 弦 marks a string

弦 (or 絃) after a number marks it as a string, and keeps it from joining the number before it. In a chord it closes the first half before the bar: 撥大九四六弦|散五 is 撥 with the thumb at hui 9.4 on string 6, and the open string 5, the same as Bv9.4,6|05.
