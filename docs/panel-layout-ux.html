Calmer Structure, movable panels

Lo-fi wireframes for the Layouts screen. Two asks: make the Structure panel less messy (mostly in Advanced), and let every panel fold up and move to the left column, the right column or the strip under the canvas. Same look as rule-builder-ux.html. This page changes no app code and no design.json.

AStructure: one line per part. What you do all the time (preview glyph, leave a part out) stays on the line. Rare edits sit behind edit ▸ on that part, and only in Advanced. Recognition gets its own small section.
BPanels: each one has the same header: ⠿ drag, ▾/▸ fold, a title, a short summary. Drag it into one of three docks. The canvas stays in the middle. Your arrangement is saved in this browser.

2 · Panels that fold and move

The Layouts screen has three docks around a fixed canvas: left column, right column, and a bottom strip under the canvas, as wide as the canvas. Each of the nine panels sits in one dock, open or folded. Nothing floats and nothing overlaps.

i · Things that change state on their own (kept to a minimum)

  • No more auto-fold. Today Structure and Centering fold when you select a part and come back when you deselect. With movable panels that would be surprising, and the new Structure is short enough to stay open. Only you fold and unfold.
  • Rule editor: opening a rule still swaps the Spacing panel's body for the editor, wherever Spacing is docked, and the canvas goes into edit mode. Glyphs and Tester grey out (but stay in place) until Confirm/Cancel, as they are hidden today.
  • "N skipped ▸" on a word type opens the skipped-shapes list inside the Word types panel, under that type, instead of at the top of the inspector.
  • Selecting a part while Spacing is folded unfolds it once, with a short highlight on its header. Folding it again sticks until the next selection.

3 · Left out on purpose, and open questions

Left out on purpose

  • Floating or detached windows; panels side by side inside one dock.
  • Tabs inside the side columns (the bottom tab bar is the only tabs).
  • Closing/hiding a panel entirely: folding is enough, and nothing can get lost.
  • Resizing single panels inside a column (the column scrolls instead).
  • Different arrangements per word type, or per screen other than Layouts.
  • Undo for panel moves (Reset covers it).
  • Merging the two Chord halves into one Structure (needs a data change).

Open questions

  • "In this word" and normal-mode Structure overlap (leave out + preview glyph). Keep both, or make In this word the folded form of Structure?
  • OK to drop the auto-fold of Structure/Centering on selection?
  • Advanced switch in the canvas toolbar: fine, or in the app header?
  • Two saved arrangements (wide/narrow), or one?
  • Should Page center be editable without Advanced, now that it has its own folded panel?
  • The tie-break list is ordered in the engine (first wins), but today's checkboxes just append in click order. The redesign shows the order and lets you drag it. Is that worth having, or is one chip per word type always enough?
