Hosted pilot: drafts, updates and review

Wireframes for docs/PILOT-UX.md. Design only: no app code or data changed. The hosting model is decided (Railway, read-only server, Supabase sign-in and drafts); this is the experience on top of it. Light theme, real names from design.json and components.csv.

One word carries the whole pilot: your draft. A tester works in their draft; the real thing is the font. Save saves the draft, Build builds from the draft, Export downloads the draft, Update brings the draft up to date with the font, and Nancy reviews a draft. When proposals come, a proposal starts life as a draft, so every one of these words stays.

  • The header always says where your work goes: a blue Your draft pill (hosted) or a dashed Local files pill (Nancy's machine). Nothing else changes between the two.
  • Everything a tester can't break stays the same as today: screens, panels, undo, ⌘S. The pilot adds a sign-in page, a few header states, two banners, three dialogs and one new screen (Review, local only).
  • The Review screen is one component used three ways: Nancy reviewing a tester's draft, a tester importing a .zip, and the "font updated" choices.

7 · Errors and empty states

7a · can't save (offline / server down)
⚠ Your draft isn't saved. We can't reach the server. Your changes are kept in this browser and will save when you're back online. Don't close this browser's private window or clear its data. Try againExport my draft

Retries every 30 s. When it works: toast "Saved to your draft" and the banner goes. Build and Tester keep working from the browser copy, with a note "using unsaved changes".

7b · saved from another tab or computer

Your draft was saved somewhere else

Another tab or computer saved your draft at 14:05, after you opened it here. Which one should stay?

Export this one firstLoad the 14:05 versionKeep this one

Same shape as today's "changed on disk" dialog. "Keep this one" replaces the other save.

7c · signed out while working
You've been signed out. Your unsaved changes are kept in this browser. Sign in again

Sign in again → back on the same page, the changes save on their own.

7d · draft restored after a crash (as today, new words)

9 · Open questions

  1. Not invited: say plainly "This email isn't on the pilot list" (proposed, friendlier) or the neutral "If you're invited, a link is on its way" (doesn't reveal who's invited)?
  2. Conflict unit: a whole word type or area is kept from one side. Is that fine for the pilot, or do you need per-rule picks inside a word type?
  3. On Apply, glyph files wait for Save with the rest (proposed). Or should they write at once, like the glyph editor's Save glyph?
  4. Review as a fifth nav item in local mode (proposed), or only ⋯ → Review a draft?
  5. Should testers get "See my changes" (read-only review of their own draft) in the pilot, or is the unsaved list enough until proposals?
  6. Does a tester's Update ever need Nancy, or are testers trusted to pick "Keep mine / Take the font's" themselves (proposed)?
